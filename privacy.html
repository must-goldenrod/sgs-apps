Privacy policy

sgs channel tools · effective 2026-11-03

sgs is a private tool used by one owner to manage their own YouTube channels. It uses the YouTube API Services. By signing in with Google you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What we access and collect

  • Google sign-in tokens for the channels the owner connects. They are stored encrypted on the owner's computer.
  • Channel data: channel ID, name, handle and subscriber count.
  • Video data for the connected channels: video IDs, titles, privacy status, publish dates, length, and statistics (views, likes, comments, shares, watch time, average view duration and percentage, subscribers gained and lost).
  • Videos and descriptions that the owner approves for upload.

We do not collect data about viewers or about any channel that is not connected by its owner. We use no cookies and no analytics on this site.

How we use it

Only to upload and schedule the owner's approved videos and to show the owner how their videos perform. The data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Storage and retention

Data stays on the owner's computer. Statistics from the YouTube API Services are kept for at most 30 days: older records are deleted every day, and only the latest value of each video, refreshed at each sync, is kept.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Revoke this tool's access at any time on your Google account's third-party access page. After that it can no longer read or upload anything.
  • To have stored data deleted, email goldenrod8888@gmail.com. We delete it within 7 days of the request.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
